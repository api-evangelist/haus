# Haus

> Haus helps brands quickly launch experiments that measure the true causal impact of their marketing. 

## Product Information
- [GeoLift] (https://www.haus.io/geolift): Haus’ experimentation platform, helping you understand the business impact of your marketing across all sales channels.
- [Causal Attribution] (https://www.haus.io/causal-attribution): Haus’ daily incrementality reporting product
- [Causal MMM] (https://www.haus.io/blog/a-first-look-at-causal-mmm): Haus’ causality-driven MMM, where incrementality experiments drive and tune the model.

## Use Cases
- [Incrementality] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/incrementality): Run holdout tests to understand how different marketing channels and tactics drive outcomes - whether that’s sales, subscriptions, or orders. 
- [Optimal Spend Level] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/optimal-spend-level): Plot your spend on a diminishing marginal return curve to pinpoint the optimal spend level of your different marketing channels.
- [Amazon and Retail] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/amazon-and-retail): Measure impact across all sales channels, including DTC, Amazon, and retail.
- [Media Mix] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/media-mix-optimization): Easily test how tactics perform across multiple channels, then calibrate your media mix modeling to drive incremental results.
- [New Channel Launch] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/new-channel-launch): Quantify the impact of launching into a new marketing channel.
- [Holidays and Promotions] (https://www.haus.io/use-cases/holidays-and-promotions): Understand how discounts and promotions affect performance, then adjust spend during holidays or key promotional periods.
